Questions

Before you book.

What does the free consultation cover?

We look at how work actually moves through your business, pick the one workflow where automation or an agent would help most, and name what should stay human. You leave with a clear recommendation, whether or not we work together.

How does outcome-based pricing work?

Discovery has a fixed fee, credited if you continue. The 90 days have a base fee, and an outcome fee you pay only if the target we wrote down together is met at day 90. Miss it, pay nothing more. The numbers are on the call and in writing before we start.

Do you build the automation and agents?

Yes. I design the workflow and set up the automation and agents in tools you own and control. If something needs a larger custom build, I bring in a builder you pay directly, and I stay on the design and the decisions.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your accounts. Nothing is used to train anything, and nothing leaves your tools without your say.

What if the team pushes back?

Then we have found the real work. Pushback is usually a question about role or trust. The team helps decide what changes, and nothing a team member tells me reaches you with their name on it.

We are bigger than 50 people.

Then the organizations page is yours. The method is the same; the number of people who have to agree is not.
