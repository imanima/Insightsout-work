You Can’t Wait for Certainty

A repeatable way to decide under pressure, one your team can use without you in the room.

Facilitated by Nima Imani and Vlad Moskovski · In person at SF Commons, San Francisco

← All events

Every big call in your org still lands on your desk. Some of it comes to you because nobody else knows what they’re allowed to decide. Some of it comes to you because handing it over feels like losing the one thing you still control. This workshop gives you a repeatable process for deciding, one your team can run without you in the room.

What we’ll do together

  1. Name the decision you’re carrying. Bring one live or recent decision. Put it on the table as it actually is, not as the tidy version.
  2. See what’s driving it. Under pressure most decisions are made from fear or from commitment. We look at which one is in the room, and what changes when you can tell the difference.
  3. Run the decision system. A short, repeatable sequence: what matters, what you know, what you don’t, who decides, when you’ll revisit. First solo, then in pairs.
  4. Make it your team’s. Work out which decisions leave your desk, and what your team needs so they can decide well without you.
  5. Leave with one decision made and a one-page process you can run on Monday.

Why this session exists

AI is making teams faster. Faster teams produce more decisions, and most of them still route through one person. When leaders wait for certainty, the team waits with them. This session is about deciding well at the speed the work now moves, and about building a team that can do the same.

Who this is for

  1. Middle managers through directors and VPs who carry more decisions than one person should.
  2. Heads of HR, People & Culture, and CHROs who help leaders decide and communicate under pressure.
  3. Founders and team leads whose team keeps waiting on them.

Bring one active or recent decision. What’s said in the room stays in the room.

What you leave with

A one-page decision process your team can use. Clarity on the decision you brought. And the experience of deciding without waiting for certainty.

Your facilitators

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He runs the weekly founder coaching group at SF Commons in San Francisco and founded InsightsOut. He co-facilitates this session with Vlad Moskovski.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco company founded by Nima Imani. It works on AI change from both sides, the people and the technology: coaching, leadership, and AI transformation. For organizations: leadership coaching and alignment, manager and team work for human–AI teams, coaching for people whose roles are changing, and the technology solution built with the team. For founders and builders: 1:1 and co-founder coaching on conflict, overwhelm, and decisions under uncertainty. For small business owners and individuals: AI transformation consulting and coaching, starting with a free consultation. For everyone: public workshops in San Francisco and research built from what people share in these rooms. One purpose underneath: protecting and strengthening human agency and leadership during rapid change.
