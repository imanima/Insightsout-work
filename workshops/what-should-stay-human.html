AI Is Changing Everything. What Should Stay Human?

A working session for anyone whose work is changing, whether or not they chose it.

Facilitated by Nima Imani · 3 hours · Frontier Tower, San Francisco · Free

← All community workshops

Something about your work is changing, and you didn't choose it. Maybe a tool now does something you were good at. Maybe everyone around you sounds certain and you don't. Maybe you feel behind. Or maybe you're excited and want to see what's going on, without the hype or the doom.

AI can play more and more of the instruments. But it can't decide what the music is for. That part is yours.

What we'll do

This is a working session, not a lecture. We slow down, look honestly at what's changing and what it brings up, remember what has always carried people through change, and find your own line: what you're willing to hand over, what should stay human, and why.

You'll leave with more clarity, one real choice made on purpose, and the experience of a room of people working this out too.

Who this is for

Small business owners, freelancers, professionals, and anyone curious or concerned. No AI experience needed.

What this is not: tool training, prompt libraries, or job-market predictions.

How Nima runs it

He won't tell you where your line should be. Real questions, slowed down, in an honest room. Half of what you take home comes from the person next to you.

Your facilitator

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He runs the weekly founder coaching group at Frontier Tower in San Francisco and founded InsightsOut.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco company founded by Nima Imani that works on the human side of AI change and leadership. It works with organizations and with individuals. For organizations, it offers leadership coaching and alignment for AI change, prepares managers and changing roles, and supports the people side of enablement. For individuals and small business owners, it runs community workshops and hands-on AI enablement. For founders, it offers coaching on co-founder and team conflict, decision uncertainty, and the emotions that come with building. Underneath everything sits one purpose: helping people protect and strengthen their human agency and leadership during rapid change, backed by research built from what people share in these rooms.
