What Should Stay Human?

A working session for anyone whose work is changing, whether or not they chose it.

Facilitated by Nima Imani · SF Commons, San Francisco

← All events

Something about your work is changing, and you didn't choose it. Maybe a tool now does something you were good at. Maybe everyone around you sounds certain and you don't. Maybe you feel behind. Or maybe you're excited and want to see what's going on, without the hype or the doom.

AI can play more and more of the instruments. But it can't decide what the music is for. That part is yours.

What we'll do

This is a working session, not a lecture. We slow down, look honestly at what's changing and what it brings up, remember what has always carried people through change, and find your own line: what you're willing to hand over, what should stay human, and why.

You'll leave with more clarity, one real choice made on purpose, and the experience of a room of people working this out too.

Who this is for

Small business owners, freelancers, professionals, and anyone curious or concerned. No AI experience needed.

What this is not: tool training, prompt libraries, or job-market predictions.

How Nima runs it

He won't tell you where your line should be. Real questions, slowed down, in an honest room. Half of what you take home comes from the person next to you.
