When Your Role Starts Changing

A workshop for navigating AI change at work, for people whose role, value, or sense of identity is shifting.

Facilitated by Nima Imani · In person at SF Commons

← All events

AI is changing more than our tools. It is changing our roles, our value, our priorities, and sometimes our sense of identity at work. This is a facilitated space to reflect, connect with peers going through the same shift, and get clear on your next steps.

How the session works

  1. Small group. Seats are intentionally limited.
  2. Guided reflection, paired conversation, facilitated group sensemaking.
  3. Practical integration you can take with you.
  4. Confidential space: stories stay, learning leaves.

Please arrive on time. The first 10 minutes matter.

Your facilitator

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He combines that background with coaching to help people slow down, see clearly, and move forward with clarity, confidence, and grounded action. He offers private coaching for working professionals, team leads, and executives and founded InsightsOut.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco practice founded by Nima Imani. We help organizations, teams, and professionals move through change with more clarity, honest communication, and practical leadership. The work includes leadership coaching, manager development, team facilitation, role change support, and AI enablement when AI is part of the change. Public workshops and living research keep the work connected to what people are actually experiencing.
