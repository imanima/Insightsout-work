Navigating Uncertainty at Work in Times of Rapid Change

A free workshop for finding clarity, values, and agency when the pace of change at work outruns you.

Facilitated by Nima Imani and Diane Friedlaender · In person in Redwood City

← All events

Are you overwhelmed by the pace and scale of change in your work? Fast change can leave you unclear about your role and short on any sense of agency over your working life. This free workshop gives you tools to untangle what is in your way, with more purpose, clarity, and power.

You'll come away with

  1. Tools for finding clarity amid rapid change.
  2. A deeper sense of your own values and inner compass.
  3. Practical strategies for reclaiming agency in your work.
  4. Connection with others navigating the same currents.

Your facilitators

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He works with professionals and leaders on uncertainty, hard decisions, and the human side of change, offers private 1:1 coaching, and founded InsightsOut.

Diane Friedlaender is an educator, facilitator, and researcher. She brings the engaged leadership and community building program she incubated at Stanford University to individuals, groups, and communities, and designs learning experiences for connection, justice, and wise leadership.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco practice founded by Nima Imani. We help organizations, teams, and professionals move through change with more clarity, honest communication, and practical leadership. The work includes leadership coaching, manager development, team facilitation, role change support, and AI enablement when AI is part of the change. Public workshops and living research keep the work connected to what people are actually experiencing.
