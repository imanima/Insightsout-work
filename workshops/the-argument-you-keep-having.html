The Argument You Keep Having

A two-hour working session for founding pairs. Map the fight you keep repeating and leave with the first draft of a working agreement.

Facilitated by Nima Imani · 2 hours · Frontier Tower Back Salon, San Francisco · $90 per person

← All community workshops

Co-founder conflict rarely starts as conflict. It starts as "we never wrote down who owns what." Then AI moves the work around. One of you picks up more, the other picks up something different, and six months later you're having the same argument for the fourth time.

What we'll do

Come together, the two of you: co-founders, or a founder and a key lead. We'll map the fight you keep repeating, name what's at stake, and you'll leave with the first draft of a working agreement on roles, decisions, money, and how you disagree.

The agenda

  1. Arrive and pressure-check.
  2. Timeline of the tension, in pairs.
  3. The loop, and what's underneath it.
  4. Roles and decisions worksheet.
  5. Draft agreement, one commitment each.

Who this is for

Two people who share a company or a team, willing to be in the room together. Register both.

What this is not for: a partnership that has already decided to split, or one founder hoping to fix the other.

Want the longer version?

Co-Founder & Team Alignment is the three-month version of this work: three months of coaching for founding pairs, ending in a signed one-page working agreement.

Your facilitator

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He runs the weekly founder coaching group at Frontier Tower in San Francisco and founded InsightsOut.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco company founded by Nima Imani that works on the human side of AI change and leadership. It works with organizations and with individuals. For organizations, it offers leadership coaching and alignment for AI change, prepares managers and changing roles, and supports the people side of enablement. For individuals and small business owners, it runs community workshops and hands-on AI enablement. For founders, it offers coaching on co-founder and team conflict, decision uncertainty, and the emotions that come with building. Underneath everything sits one purpose: helping people protect and strengthen their human agency and leadership during rapid change, backed by research built from what people share in these rooms.
