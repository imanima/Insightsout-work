My Team Is Overwhelmed. How Do I Lead Through Uncertainty?

A sensemaking session for managers caught between the push for speed and a team that's stretched thin.

Facilitated by Nima Imani · 90 minutes · Frontier Tower, San Francisco · Free

← All community workshops

Leadership wants speed: AI, new tools, more output with the same people. Your team wants clarity, a say, and more time. You're in the middle, doing most of the deciding, communicating, and steadying.

What we'll do together

  1. Put down what you're carrying. Map what lands on you from above, what rises from below, and the part you never say at work.
  2. Be heard without being fixed. In pairs: one person speaks, one reflects. No advice.
  3. Read your team differently. Resistance is usually information. Map who's pushing back, who's gone quiet, who's quietly ahead, and what each of them might be protecting.
  4. Practice the honest thing. Draft what you'd actually say to your team: what you know, what you don't, and what won't change. Say it out loud. Notice which sentence sounds like a press release, and find the truer one.
  5. Leave with one conversation you'll have in the next two weeks.

Why this session exists

In registration data from our last eight workshops, 361 people, managers were the only group where trust, resistance, and communication came up for more than half. And what managers asked for most was time with peers, not a framework.

Who this is for

  1. Middle managers and team leads in any function.
  2. Managers leading teams through AI rollouts, restructuring, new systems, fast growth, or shifting priorities.
  3. People leaders holding the team's worries while staying accountable for delivery.

Bring one real situation. What's said in the room stays in the room.

Your facilitator

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He runs the weekly founder coaching group at Frontier Tower in San Francisco and founded InsightsOut.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco company founded by Nima Imani that works on the human side of AI change and leadership. It works with organizations and with individuals. For organizations, it offers leadership coaching and alignment for AI change, prepares managers and changing roles, and supports the people side of enablement. For individuals and small business owners, it runs community workshops and hands-on AI enablement. For founders, it offers coaching on co-founder and team conflict, decision uncertainty, and the emotions that come with building. Underneath everything sits one purpose: helping people protect and strengthen their human agency and leadership during rapid change, backed by research built from what people share in these rooms.
