Founder Group Coaching & Peer Support

Weekly small-group coaching for founders. Bring one real situation and take a useful next step.

Facilitated by Nima Imani · Weekly, Fridays 2:00 PM · SF Commons, San Francisco · $10, free for members

← All events

Building a company can be exciting and deeply lonely. Some weeks the hardest part is a strained relationship with a co-founder or team member. Other weeks it's the uncertainty, the overwhelm, and the pressure of deciding without all the answers.

How it works

This is a weekly gathering where founders bring one real situation, receive coaching, learn from one another, and take a useful next step.

It's not a lecture and it's not a networking event. It's facilitated small-group coaching around real situations. You can participate and receive coaching, offer peer reflection, or just listen. No names or confidential details needed.

Two connected themes

  1. Relationships under pressure: co-founder tension, team conflict, difficult conversations, trust, roles, boundaries, working agreements.
  2. Clarity under pressure: uncertainty, overwhelm, competing priorities, difficult decisions, attention, energy.

Want to go deeper?

The group is also the front door to 1:1 and co-founder coaching.

The details

  • Format: Facilitated small-group coaching
  • When: Weekly, Fridays at 2:00 PM
  • Location: SF Commons, North Studio, 550 Laguna St, San Francisco
  • Price: $10 · free for SF Commons members

Your facilitator

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He runs the weekly founder coaching group at SF Commons in San Francisco and founded InsightsOut.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco company founded by Nima Imani that works on the human side of AI change and leadership. For organizations: leadership coaching and alignment, manager and team work for human–AI teams, coaching for people whose roles are changing, and the technology solution built with the team. For founders and builders: 1:1 and co-founder coaching on conflict, overwhelm, and decisions under uncertainty. For small business owners and individuals: AI transformation consulting and coaching, starting with a free consultation. For everyone: public workshops in San Francisco and research built from what people share in these rooms. One purpose underneath: protecting and strengthening human agency and leadership during rapid change.
