Community workshop

Founder Group Coaching & Peer Support

Free weekly small-group coaching for founders. Bring one real situation and take a useful next step.

Facilitated by Nima Imani · Weekly, Fridays 2:00 PM · Frontier Tower Back Salon, San Francisco · Free

← All community workshops

Building a company can be exciting and deeply lonely. Some weeks the hardest part is a strained relationship with a co-founder or team member. Other weeks it's the uncertainty, the overwhelm, and the pressure of deciding without all the answers.

How it works

This is a free weekly gathering where founders bring one real situation, receive coaching, learn from one another, and take a useful next step.

It's not a lecture and it's not a networking event. It's facilitated small-group coaching around real situations. You can participate and receive coaching, offer peer reflection, or just listen. No names or confidential details needed.

Two connected themes

  1. Relationships under pressure: co-founder tension, team conflict, difficult conversations, trust, roles, boundaries, working agreements.
  2. Clarity under pressure: uncertainty, overwhelm, competing priorities, difficult decisions, attention, energy.

Want to go deeper?

The group is also the front door to 1:1 and co-founder coaching.

The details

  • When: Weekly, Fridays at 2:00 PM
  • Location: Frontier Tower Back Salon, 995 Market Street, San Francisco

Your facilitator

Nima Imani is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than ten years in software, data and AI, including EY and Neo4j. He runs the weekly founder coaching group at Frontier Tower in San Francisco and founded InsightsOut.

About InsightsOut

InsightsOut (insightsout.work) is a San Francisco company founded by Nima Imani that works on the human side of AI change and leadership. It works with organizations and with individuals. For organizations, it helps leadership teams align on AI, prepares managers whose teams are stretched, and runs AI enablement labs on real work. For people, it runs community workshops and coaches founders and leaders through decision uncertainty, co-founder conflict, and changing roles. Underneath both sits one purpose: helping people protect and strengthen their human agency and leadership during rapid change, backed by research built from what people share in these rooms.
