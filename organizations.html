What we see

The change is human before it is technical.

Conflict at the top

Leaders disagree about how fast to go, what AI is for, and who owns it. The disagreement rarely gets said out loud, so it shows up downstream as a rollout that stalls.

Agents as teammates

People are now expected to work alongside AI agents. Nobody has said what their role is now, what the agent’s is, or what good work looks like.

Managers in the middle

Managers are asked to deliver the change and hold the team together, often while quietly feeling left behind by AI themselves. They need language and skills for conversations they have never had to have.

A team hearing different messages

Adoption becomes real when the whole team is aligned on what is changing and why. Until then it stays a mandate people work around.

People whose jobs are changing

Someone’s role is shifting under them. That is personal before it is operational, and it needs a place to be worked through.

What we do

Three kinds of work. Usually in this order.

Leadership coaching and alignment

Coaching for the leaders steering the change, and facilitated sessions that turn different views at the top into one position the whole team can act on: priorities, ownership, and what stays human.

Manager and team work

Workshops and coaching for managers leading human–AI teams: naming role changes honestly, communicating under pressure, and leading people through uncertainty and resistance.

Coaching for people in role change

1:1 coaching, sponsored by the organization, for individuals whose roles are changing because of AI.

Common questions

What does InsightsOut do for organizations?

We work on the human side of AI change inside organizations: coaching and alignment for the leadership team, workshops and coaching for managers leading human–AI teams, and 1:1 coaching for people whose roles are changing. The goal is one aligned team that can lead the change, not a rollout that stalls in the middle.

How is this different from AI training?

AI training teaches tools. We work on what training cannot fix: disagreement at the top about what AI is for, managers under pressure without the language to lead it, and people whose roles are changing without anyone saying so. Alignment first, then the tools land.

How does an engagement start?

With one facilitated Leadership Conversation, in the San Francisco Bay Area or online. You continue only where the work needs it.
