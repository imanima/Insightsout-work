How it works

Start with leadership. Enable through real work.

When AI rollouts start with training, they often stall on people. Begin with one facilitated leadership session; continue only where needed.

01

90 minutes to half day · Leadership team

Surface what AI is changing for the work and the people—including concerns that are not being said openly.

Common questions

Questions organizations ask.

What does InsightsOut do for organizations?

InsightsOut helps leadership teams work through the human side of AI change: a Leadership Conversation to surface what AI is changing, Leadership Alignment to turn differing opinions into one position, Manager and Role-Change Workshops for the people affected, and an AI Enablement Lab that applies those decisions to one real workflow.

How is this different from AI training?

AI training teaches tools. InsightsOut starts with leadership alignment, role clarity, and what should stay human, then enables real work. Rollouts that start with training tend to stall on people; this sequence is designed to prevent that.

Where do you work and how do engagements start?

San Francisco Bay Area and online. Every engagement starts with a single facilitated Leadership Conversation (90 minutes to half a day); you continue only where it is useful.

Who leads the work?

Nima Imani, founder of InsightsOut — an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than a decade in software, data, and AI (EY, Neo4j) — together with coaches and facilitators from technology, product, operations, and organizational development.
