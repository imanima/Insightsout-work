How it works

From leadership clarity to one tested practice.

When AI change starts with tools and training, the unresolved leadership questions move downstream. Begin with one facilitated conversation; continue only where the work needs it.

01

Leadership Conversation

90 minutes to half day · Leadership team

Surface what AI is changing for the work and the people—including concerns that are not being said openly.

You leave with: a shared map of the change, the pressure points, and the next decision.

02

Leadership Alignment

Typically 2–3 working sessions

Turn different opinions about AI into one leadership position people can act on.

You leave with: priorities, ownership, boundaries, and clear principles for what should stay human.

03

Manager and Role-Change Workshops

Managers and affected teams

Help managers name role changes honestly and lead through overwhelm, uncertainty, and resistance.

You leave with: managers with useful language and a plan; teams with space to process the change.

04

AI Integration Lab

Managers and teams · Several weeks

Choose one real workflow. Decide where AI helps, where human judgment stays, and test the new process with the people doing the work.

You leave with: one tested AI-supported workflow, clear review rules, and evidence about what to keep, change, or scale.

The lab comes last on purpose. It turns leadership decisions into practice without asking teams to absorb another disconnected tool rollout.

Common questions

Questions organizations ask.

What does InsightsOut do for organizations?

InsightsOut helps leadership teams work through the human side of AI change: surface what is changing, align on the decisions, prepare managers and affected teams, and test one responsible AI-supported workflow through an integration lab.

How is this different from AI training?

AI training teaches tools. InsightsOut starts with leadership alignment, role clarity, and what should stay human. The final lab applies those decisions to one real workflow with clear human ownership.

Where do you work and how do engagements start?

San Francisco Bay Area and online. Every engagement starts with a single facilitated Leadership Conversation (90 minutes to half a day); you continue only where it is useful.

Who leads the work?

Nima Imani leads the work. He is an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than a decade across software, data, AI, and organizational transformation, including EY and Neo4j. Additional specialists are brought in only when the work requires them.
