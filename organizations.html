The human side of AI change

AI change is a leadership challenge before it is a training challenge.

We start with the leadership team: make sense of what AI is changing, align on what should stay human, and prepare managers to lead the transition. Enablement follows.

How it works

Start with leadership. Enable through real work.

When AI rollouts start with training, they often stall on people. Begin with one facilitated leadership session; continue only where needed.

01

Leadership Conversation

90 minutes to half day · Leadership team

Surface what AI is changing for the work and the people—including concerns that are not being said openly.

You leave with: a shared map of the change, the pressure points, and the next decision.

Start here →
02

Leadership Alignment

Typically 2–3 working sessions

Turn different opinions about AI into one leadership position people can act on.

You leave with: priorities, ownership, boundaries, and clear principles for what should stay human.

03

Manager and Role-Change Workshops

Managers and affected teams

Help managers name role changes honestly and lead through overwhelm, uncertainty, and resistance.

You leave with: managers with useful language and a plan; teams with space to process the change.

04

AI Enablement Lab

One team · One workflow · Several weeks

Apply the leadership choices to real work with clear human ownership and review. Test, learn, and adjust.

You leave with: a tested practice, evidence of what works, and a grounded decision about what to scale.

Lab details →

The Lab is the last step on purpose. Enablement without alignment is how rollouts stall.
