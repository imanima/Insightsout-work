For executive, People, and transformation leaders

What 10x actually means

The work speeds up. The team has to keep up.

10x more decisions

Every agent output is a decision someone has to own. Without clarity on who decides what, and on what basis, the work gets faster and the team gets slower.

10x more communication

More handoffs, more context to share, more to align on. Managers carry most of it, often while quietly feeling left behind by AI themselves.

Leadership in every seat

Agents get onboarded like teammates. That takes leadership from everyone on the team, not only the top, and a team aligned on what is changing and why.

Roles that already changed

Someone’s job shifted under them and nobody said so. That is personal before it is operational, and it needs a place to be worked through.

Conflict at the top

Leaders disagree about how fast to go, what AI is for, and who owns it. The disagreement rarely gets said out loud, so it shows up downstream as a rollout that stalls.

What we do

Four kinds of work. Usually in this order.

Leadership coaching and alignment

Coaching for the leaders steering the change, and facilitated sessions that turn different views at the top into one position the whole team can act on: priorities, ownership, and what stays human.

Manager and team work

Workshops and coaching for managers and teams: onboarding agents as teammates, deciding who decides what, communicating under pressure, and leading people through uncertainty and resistance.

Coaching for people in role change

1:1 coaching, sponsored by the organization, for individuals whose roles are changing because of AI.

The technology solution

We design and build the agent setup with your team, so the people and the technology change together instead of one waiting on the other.

Common questions

What does InsightsOut do for organizations?

We help organizations run as human–AI teams: coaching and alignment for the leadership team, workshops and coaching for managers and teams onboarding agents as teammates, 1:1 coaching for people whose roles are changing, and the technology solution built with your team. The goal is one aligned team that can carry 10x more decisions and communication, not a rollout that stalls in the middle.

How is this different from AI training?

AI training teaches tools. We work on what training cannot fix: disagreement at the top about what AI is for, the clarity a team needs when decisions multiply, managers under pressure without the language to lead it, and people whose roles have changed without anyone saying so. Alignment first, then the technology lands.

How does an engagement start?

With one facilitated Leadership Conversation, in the San Francisco Bay Area or online. You continue only where the work needs it.
