How it works

Three months. One real situation.

I coach two things at once: the outside goal (the promotion, the decision, the customers, the hard conversation) and the inside shift, who you need to become to get there.

  1. Free 30 minute call. We talk about what is happening and whether working together makes sense.
  2. Three months of coaching. We set the outside goal and name the inside shift. Every session ends with something you will do that week.
  3. Review and decide. We look at what changed, outside and inside, and decide what comes next.
