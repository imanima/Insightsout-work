Before we meet

Start with a real conversation.

What happens on the first call?

We spend 30 minutes talking about what you’re navigating, what you want to change, and whether working together feels useful. There is no obligation to enroll.

Do I have to choose private or group coaching first?

No. We can explore both on the call and decide which format fits your goals and circumstances.

Is coaching confidential?

Private coaching conversations stay between us, subject to the boundaries in our coaching agreement. If an employer pays, we agree in advance what administrative information they receive.

Can my company pay?
