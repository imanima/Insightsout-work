What brings you here

Start with the situation, not your title.

You don’t need a perfect goal before we talk. You only need one real situation that deserves your attention.

01

Your role or career is changing

You are rethinking what comes next, stepping into more responsibility, or trying to find where your experience still matters. We turn uncertainty into a direction you can test.

02

Your leadership is being tested

Your team is stretched, a relationship is difficult, or the decisions keep getting heavier. We work on judgment, communication, boundaries, and the next conversation.

03

You are carrying the business

You are holding the product, the team, and decisions few people around you can fully understand. We create space for focus, honest reflection, and action.

Technology and AI may be changing the work around you, but AI doesn’t need to be the reason you come.

Why work with me

I know the work around the person.

I spent more than a decade across engineering, data, AI, solution architecture, and technology leadership. At EY and Neo4j, I helped people make complex technology useful. As a founder and CTO, I built products and led a team through uncertainty and change.

Today, I bring that operating experience together with professional coaching. I won’t pretend to know your answer. I will help you see the situation more clearly, notice what pressure is doing, and choose a next step that is yours.

Yes. What we discuss stays between us. If your employer sponsors the work, we agree in advance on the goals and what, if anything, is shared outside the sessions.

Can my employer pay?

Possibly. Many organizations have coaching, leadership development, learning, or professional development budgets. I can provide the documents you need. See the employer guide →

Where do we meet?

Online or in person in San Francisco. The first call is 30 minutes and free. We use it to understand what you are facing and whether working together makes sense.

Not sure where to start?
