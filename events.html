The four series

Same title everywhere. Same promise.

Founder Group Coaching & Peer Support

A weekly room for founders carrying difficult decisions, relationship tension, and the weight of building. Bring one real situation. Be coached, reflect for someone else, or listen. No pitching.

You leave with clarity on one real situation and a supported next move. The front door to 1:1 and co-founder coaching.

Every Friday · 2:00 PM · SF Commons, 550 Laguna St, North Studio · Free for members, $10 for others

What Should Stay Human?

A small-group conversation about what we want to protect, practice, and choose as AI changes work and life. Not a debate about whether AI is good or bad, and not a technology class. Worried or excited, experienced or new to AI, you belong in the room.

You leave with more clarity about your own relationship with AI, one choice made on purpose, and a wider view of how other people are drawing their lines.

Saturday, October 10 · 2:00 PM · SF Commons, 540 Laguna St · Free for members, $10 suggested for othersNext date on Luma

When Agents Join the Team

A working session about trust, accountability, and decision-making in human-agent teams. Who decides? Who checks the work? Who answers when an agent gets it wrong? What should always stay human? Bring one real workflow.

You leave with a one-page Human-Agent Team Agreement, clearer decision rights, and a review practice the team can repeat. Runs inside companies on request.

When the Work Changes

For people whose roles are changing and managers expected to lead others through the same uncertainty. The room begins together, then splits into two working paths: role and identity, or management and team leadership.

You leave with a clear map of the change you are navigating, one conversation or decision to move forward, and one capability or experiment to begin building. Our two largest rooms so far.
