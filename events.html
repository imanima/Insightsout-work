What to expect

Bring one real question.

01

Start where you are

You do not need a polished story or a clear answer. Bring what is actually happening.

02

Think with other people

Listen, reflect, and share as much or as little as you choose. These are facilitated conversations, not networking events.

03

Leave with a next step

The aim is not to solve everything. It is to see the situation more clearly and choose one useful move.

Continue the work

A gathering can be a beginning.

If you want more continuity, continue in a small coaching group or bring the situation into a private conversation.
