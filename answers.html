Who is private coaching for?

Professionals, managers, executives, and founders facing a difficult decision, role or career change, team pressure, or the demands of building a business. The starting engagement is a three month program with six sessions and support between sessions. Start with a free 30 minute call.

Can my employer pay for coaching?

Often, yes. Check your leadership development, learning, coaching, or professional development budget. We can provide a program description, invoice, and receipt. Your employer’s policy determines reimbursement, and your coaching conversations remain confidential.

Can I use my learning stipend or wellness account for coaching?

Often, yes. Many learning and development stipends name coaching, and some wellness or lifestyle accounts list life or wellbeing coaching. Check your policy for "coaching" or "professional development". Every package comes with an itemized invoice built for reimbursement.

How do I ask my manager to pay for coaching?

Keep it short: what it is, what it costs, what you will get from it. There are copy-ready email and Slack templates, plus one for asking a manager or learning team to sponsor a longer engagement.
