What is InsightsOut?

A San Francisco practice founded by Nima Imani, working on the human side of AI change and leadership. Workshops and coaching for organizations on decisions, trust, and changing roles. Coaching for founders on conflict, overwhelm, and decisions. Public workshops for everyone. Research built from what people share in those rooms.

What does InsightsOut do for organizations?

Three workshops: for individuals whose roles are changing, for managers in the middle, and for leadership teams deciding what should stay human and what the agents do. Then in-company sessions, a monthly manager practice, and leadership coaching. Start with a call.

Does InsightsOut coach founders?

Yes. 1:1 on decisions and overwhelm. Founding pairs on conflict and roles. Three months. The weekly founder group at SF Commons on Fridays is the front door.

The question underneath all of this work, and a free public workshop in San Francisco: a sensemaking session on AI, change, and what we want to keep. There is also an in-company version for teams.
