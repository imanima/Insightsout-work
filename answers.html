InsightsOut is a San Francisco practice founded by Nima Imani. Who is Nima Imani?

Nima Imani is the founder of InsightsOut and an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach. He spent more than a decade in software, data, AI, solution architecture, and organizational transformation, including EY and Neo4j. He coaches founders and founding teams, runs a weekly founder group in San Francisco, and leads InsightsOut’s organizational and AI transformation work.

What does InsightsOut do for organizations?

It helps organizations run as human–AI teams. 10x productivity means 10x more decisions and 10x more communication. The work is leadership alignment, manager and team sessions, coaching for people whose roles are changing, and the technology solution built with the team. Every engagement starts with one Leadership Conversation.

Does InsightsOut coach founders?

Yes. Two three-month programs: From Uncertainty to Clear Decisions (1:1) and Co-Founder & Team Alignment (founding pairs). The work is conflict, overwhelm, and decisions under uncertainty, aimed at product-market fit, the raise, and revenue. Start with a free 30-minute call. There is also a weekly founder group at SF Commons.

What does InsightsOut do for small businesses?

AI transformation consulting and coaching for owners, coaches, consultants, and individuals who feel left behind on AI: more revenue, less time and money wasted. A free 30-minute consultation puts numbers on it first. Then working automations are built together, and the owner is coached to run them.

How do I start?

Organizations book a Leadership Conversation. Founders book a free 30-minute coaching call. Small business owners and individuals book a free consultation. Anyone can come to a public workshop. Pricing for ongoing work is discussed on the call, not published here.
