A San Francisco practice founded by Nima Imani, working on the human side of AI change and leadership. Workshops and coaching for organizations on decisions, trust, and changing roles. What does InsightsOut do for organizations?

Three problems: human-agent teams, role change, and leadership under uncertainty. Two workshops that run in public and inside companies: When Agents Join the Team, which ends in a one-page Human-Agent Team Agreement, and When the Work Changes, which ends in a change map, one conversation, and one experiment. Then leadership coaching, a monthly manager practice, and a bounded AI Enablement Lab, last on purpose. Start with the smallest useful step, usually one workshop with one team.

Does InsightsOut coach founders?

Yes. 1:1 on decisions and overwhelm. Founding pairs on conflict and roles. Three months. The weekly founder group at SF Commons on Fridays is the front door.

What is What Should Stay Human?

The question underneath all of this work, a free public workshop in San Francisco, and the name of the newsletter. The workshop is a small-group conversation about what we want to protect, practice, and choose as AI changes work and life. The workplace version is When Agents Join the Team.

What are the four InsightsOut events?

Founder Group Coaching & Peer Support, weekly on Fridays at SF Commons. What Should Stay Human?, a public sensemaking session. When Agents Join the Team, a working session on trust, accountability, and decision rights in human-agent teams. When the Work Changes, for people whose roles are changing and the managers leading them. Each returns regularly; dates are on Luma.
