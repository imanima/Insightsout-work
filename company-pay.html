Where the money usually is

Three places to look.

Learning or development stipend

A yearly amount for courses, conferences, and growth. Many policies name coaching. Others leave it open to anything that helps you grow at work.

Wellness or lifestyle account

Often run through Forma, Benepass, or Compt. Some list life or wellbeing coaching as an eligible category. Check the categories in your app.

Your manager or learning team

For longer work, the cost can come from your team's budget or a leadership development program. I can invoice your company directly once it is approved.

A few published examples, as of October 2026: Adobe's professional development reimbursement lists executive coaching. Microsoft's Perks+ lists wellbeing coaching. GitLab funds professional coaching for managers and leaders. The City of San Francisco's MEA training fund lists executive coaching. Policies change, so always check your current one.

Claiming

How it usually works on common platforms.

Compt

Upload the invoice. Compt looks for the vendor, the date, and the total. Some companies limit how early a receipt can be dated.

Forma

Submit a claim with the provider, a short description, and the service dates. The invoice covers all three.

Benepass

The card may work at checkout. If it is declined, submit a reimbursement with the invoice, or ask support to approve the merchant.

Your company's own portal

Many companies run their own reimbursement. Check whether your manager needs to approve first, and when claims close for the year.

Year-end matters. Many budgets reset in January and do not roll over. Adobe's professional development claims must be approved by December 30. Microsoft Perks+ claims for the year are due by January 31.

Questions

Before you ask.

Can my learning stipend pay for coaching?

Often, yes. Many learning and development stipends name coaching, and many others cover anything that helps you grow at work. Check your policy for the words "coaching" or "professional development", then ask your manager with the template above.

Can I use a wellness or lifestyle spending account?

Sometimes. Some wellness accounts list life or wellbeing coaching as an eligible category. The categories are set by your employer, so check your Forma, Benepass, or Compt app before booking.

What if my budget is smaller than the package?

Tell me your budget on the free call and we will shape the work to fit it. Some managers cover the difference from their team budget.

What if my company says no?

Ask what would qualify instead. Sometimes it is the wording, sometimes the category. If it still does not fit, we can talk about paying personally, with split payments.

Does my employer learn what we talk about?

No. Session content stays confidential. If your company sponsors the coaching, you, the sponsor, and I agree at the start on the goals and on what, if anything, is shared.

I work for the federal government. Can my agency pay?

Federal guidance treats coaching as a development activity. You can request it through an SF-182 training request with your supervisor's approval. I can provide what the form needs.
