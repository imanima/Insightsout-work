In short

What is InsightsOut?

InsightsOut is a San Francisco company founded by Nima Imani. It works on AI change from both sides, the people and the technology: coaching, leadership, and AI transformation. For organizations: leadership coaching and alignment, manager and team work for human–AI teams, coaching for people whose roles are changing, and the technology solution built with the team. For founders and builders: 1:1 and co-founder coaching on conflict, overwhelm, and decisions under uncertainty. For small business owners and individuals: AI transformation consulting and coaching, starting with a free consultation. For everyone: public workshops and research. One purpose underneath: protecting and strengthening human agency and leadership during rapid change.

Who is Nima Imani?

Nima Imani is the founder of InsightsOut and an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than a decade in software, data, AI, solution architecture, and organizational transformation, including work at EY and Neo4j. He is based in San Francisco, coaches founders and founding teams, runs a weekly founder coaching group, and leads InsightsOut’s organizational work and research.

Is InsightsOut the same as the InsightsOut emotional learning app?

No. The InsightsOut emotional learning app was an earlier startup that closed in 2026; only the name carried over. Today’s InsightsOut does coaching, leadership, and AI transformation work with organizations, founders, small businesses, and individuals, in San Francisco and online.

Where do you work, and how do I start?

San Francisco and online. Organizations start with one Leadership Conversation; founders start with a free 30-minute call; small business owners and individuals start with a free consultation; anyone can come to a workshop listed on Luma.
