The thread through my work

Good technology needs people behind it.

I’ve been a teacher, engineer, solution architect, and founder. The thread is helping people understand change and take part in it.

At EY and Neo4j, I worked across enterprise technology, customer discovery, executive workshops, and adoption planning.

The technology is only part of it. People also need shared expectations, trust, and clear responsibility.

As a founder, I know the uncertainty firsthand. As an ICF Associate Certified Coach, I help others work through it.

Why InsightsOut exists

We make sense of change together.

AI and other changes are reshaping work, relationships, communities, and how people understand their own value. There is a lot to take in.

Through coaching, events, and research, we create room to listen, feel supported, and choose how to move forward.
