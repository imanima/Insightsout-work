The thread through my work

Helping people see a little more clearly.

My work has moved through teaching, technology, leadership, and entrepreneurship. The thread has always been people.

I spent more than a decade working across engineering, data, solution architecture, and enterprise change, including at EY and Neo4j. I’ve been a founder and CTO, and I know the uncertainty of building something without having every answer.

I’m an ICF Associate Certified Coach with ontological coach training. I pay attention to how we interpret a situation, what it brings up in us, and the choices that become available when we slow down enough to see it.

I bring experience and thoughtful questions. You bring your own knowledge of your life. We work from there.

Why InsightsOut exists

We’re not meant to make sense of it all alone.

AI and other changes are reshaping work, relationships, communities, and how people understand their own value. There is a lot to take in.

Through coaching, events, and research, InsightsOut creates space to feel heard, listen to others, and act with greater clarity and agency.
