The thread through my work

A good solution has to work for people, too.

My work has moved through teaching, engineering, enterprise technology, and founding a company. The question keeps coming back: what helps people understand the change and take part in it?

At EY, I worked on technical delivery in complex transformation environments. At Neo4j, I worked with enterprise customers on discovery, executive workshops, solution strategy, proofs of concept, and adoption planning.

That experience taught me to look beyond technical correctness. People can have the same tools and different ideas about success, responsibility, or what they are being asked to give up.

Building a company brought those questions into my own life. I know what it is to make decisions without every answer, and to need other people while doing it.

Why InsightsOut exists

We’re not meant to make sense of it all alone.

AI and other changes are reshaping work, relationships, communities, and how people understand their own value. There is a lot to take in.

Through coaching, events, and research, InsightsOut creates space to feel heard, listen to others, and act with greater clarity and agency.

What I’m exploring

How do we keep our agency as work changes?

AI raises questions about what we can do, what we should delegate, and how we want to live and work together. I explore those questions through writing, workshops, and research drawn from optional event registration responses.
