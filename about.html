Why InsightsOut

AI training is not enough.

AI is moving faster than most organizations and people can make sense of it. New tools change roles, decisions, relationships, and how people understand their own value—not only how tasks get done.

The work begins with honest leadership conversations and alignment. It continues by preparing managers and teams, and becomes real when people apply what they have learned to their actual work.

The practice

Technology experience. Human depth.

InsightsOut was founded by Nima Imani, an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than a decade across software, data, AI, solution architecture, and organizational transformation—including work at EY and Neo4j.

The practice brings together coaches and facilitators with backgrounds in technology, product, operations, and organizational development. That combination lets us work with both the systems being changed and the people changing them.

Three quick answers.

Who is Nima Imani?

Nima Imani is the founder of InsightsOut and an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than a decade in software, data, AI, solution architecture, and organizational transformation, including work at EY and Neo4j. He is based in San Francisco, coaches founders and founding teams, runs a free weekly founder coaching group at Frontier Tower, and leads InsightsOut's leadership work and research.

What is InsightsOut?

InsightsOut is a San Francisco practice working on the human side of AI change. It helps leadership teams align on AI and role change, supports managers and teams through workshops and enablement labs, coaches founders on decision overwhelm and co-founder alignment, runs community workshops, and publishes living research built from workshop registrations.

Where do you work, and how do I start?

San Francisco and online. Organizations start with one Leadership Conversation; founders start with a free 30-minute call; anyone can come to a workshop or the Friday founder group listed on Luma.
