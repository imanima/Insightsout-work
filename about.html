About InsightsOut

Technology changes fast. People change at a human pace.

InsightsOut is a leadership practice for the age of AI. We combine change management, leadership coaching, and team enablement to help organizations move through AI change with their people, not past them.

Meet the Practice

What we do

Change work that starts with leaders, not tools.

Most AI initiatives begin with training and stall on people. We work in the opposite order: first helping leadership teams make sense of what AI is changing, then aligning on priorities, responsibilities, and what should stay human.

From there we prepare managers and teams to lead the transition — through workshops, role-change support, and coaching — and only then move to enablement: applying the decisions to real workflows and learning from actual use.

The work is grounded on both sides. Our roots are in software, data, and enterprise AI transformation, and our method comes from professional coaching and facilitation. We understand the systems being changed and the people changing them.

The team

A team of leadership coaches and facilitators.

InsightsOut engagements are delivered by a group of professional leadership coaches and facilitators — ICF-certified, trained in ontological and executive coaching, with prior careers across technology, product, operations, and organizational development.

Every engagement follows one method: sense-making before alignment, alignment before enablement. Coaches are matched to your context — a technical organization gets coaches who have shipped software; a leadership team in transition gets coaches who have led through one.

Coaching is confidential. No notes are shared and there is no employer reporting on individual sessions — which is what makes leaders willing to bring the real thing.

What we believe

Transformation becomes real when people can understand what is changing, talk honestly about it, and shape what happens next.

The founder

Founded at the intersection of technology and human change.

InsightsOut was founded by Nima Imani, an ICF-certified, ontologically trained coach with more than a decade in software engineering, data, AI, and enterprise transformation — including digital transformation work at EY and customer-facing solution work at Neo4j.

That career taught him that transformation is never only technical: new tools change how people make decisions, work together, and understand their own value. InsightsOut brings those two bodies of work together — deep experience with technology, and close attention to how people actually move through change.

Nima leads the practice from San Francisco, where InsightsOut also hosts a community of leaders navigating AI-era change.
