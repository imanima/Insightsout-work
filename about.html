About InsightsOut

The human side of AI change.

InsightsOut is a San Francisco practice founded by Nima Imani. Workshops and coaching for organizations on decisions, trust, and changing roles. Coaching for founders on conflict, overwhelm, and decisions. Public workshops for everyone, and research built from what people share in those rooms.
