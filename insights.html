How we do research

Small questions. Careful claims.

Full method, limits, and what we won't publish: methodology →

  1. Ask at registration. Optional questions about change, challenges, and desired support. Wording and question sets varied across the first-edition events.
  2. Code the answers. Free text is themed by hand with a conservative mapping across events; role and segment are kept where captured.
  3. Cross-check. Compare with external research as context, while retaining the limits of this sample.
  4. Publish with a version. State sample, date range, limits, and corrections. Separate new data editions from editorial changes; use only aggregated or anonymized material.
