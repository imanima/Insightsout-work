What people say when they come to me

  • “I'm busy all day and still behind.”

What we work on

The outside goal and the inside shift, at the same time.

The outside goal

  • Pick the one priority that matters this quarter, and say it out loud.
  • Decide what to drop, delegate, or hand back.
  • Set a weekly rhythm you can keep, with real time off.

The inside shift

  • Notice what makes everything feel urgent.
  • Say no without guilt, and let some things stay unfinished.
  • Stop measuring your worth by how much you carry.

You leave with

One clear priority, a short list of what to drop or hand off, and a weekly rhythm that protects your energy.

Why me for this

I study this, and I've lived it.

In my research with leaders at InsightsOut events, more than 70% said they feel overwhelmed. I have also carried the founder load myself: too many priorities, not enough people, and every decision landing on me. I know what it looks like from the inside, and what helped me get out of it.

How it works

Three months. One real situation.

We focus on the situation in front of you, and the patterns shaping how you approach it. Then you test what changes in real work.

  1. Free 30 minute call. We talk about what is happening and whether working together makes sense.
  2. Three months of coaching. We set the outside goal and name the inside shift. Every session ends with something you will do that week.
  3. Review and decide. We look at what changed, outside and inside, and decide what comes next.
