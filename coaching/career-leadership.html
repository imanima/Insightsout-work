What we work on

The outside goal and the inside shift, at the same time.

The outside goal

  • Get the promotion, or a clear answer on why not.
  • Decide whether to stay, move, or go build the side project.
  • Lead your team through change without losing people.

The inside shift

  • Speak up for your work without feeling like you are bragging.
  • Move from doing the work to leading the people who do it.
  • Make the decision from what you want, not from fear.

You leave with

A clear next move (the promotion, a new role, or your side project) and the confidence and conversations to make it happen.

Why me for this

I've been the IC, the lead, and the manager.

I started as an engineer, became a tech lead and solution architect, and later built and led my own team as a CTO. At EY and Neo4j I worked inside large organizations going through technical change, and saw how it lands on managers and the people they lead: technical first, then managerial.

How it works

Three months. One real situation.

We focus on the situation in front of you, and the patterns shaping how you approach it. Then you test what changes in real work.

  1. Free 30 minute call. We talk about what is happening and whether working together makes sense.
  2. Three months of coaching. We set the outside goal and name the inside shift. Every session ends with something you will do that week.
  3. Review and decide. We look at what changed, outside and inside, and decide what comes next.

The program

Six private sessions

  • Six 60 minute sessions over three months
  • Two sessions each month, message support in between
  • A shared goals page we both update
