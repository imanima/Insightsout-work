What we work on

The outside goal and the inside shift, at the same time.

The outside goal

  • Get to your next customers with one focused path, not five.
  • Reach an honest read on product-market fit.
  • Make the decisions you have been putting off: roles, hires, focus.

The inside shift

  • Have the conversation with your co-founder you have been avoiding.
  • Stop deciding everything alone.
  • Lead from conviction instead of second-guessing.

You leave with

Co-founders on the same page, one focused path to your next customers, and decisions you stop second-guessing. Co-founders can do joint sessions together.

Why me for this

Since then I have coached 100+ founders and business owners, and run a founder group coaching series for two years. I know product and engineering well enough to follow the details, and you stay the one who decides.

How it works

Three months. One real situation.

We focus on the situation in front of you, and the patterns shaping how you approach it. Then you test what changes in real work.

  1. Free 30 minute call. We talk about what is happening and whether working together makes sense.
  2. Three months of coaching. We set the outside goal and name the inside shift. Every session ends with something you will do that week.
  3. Review and decide. We look at what changed, outside and inside, and decide what comes next.
