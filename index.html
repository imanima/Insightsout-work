The question behind the work

What changes when AI joins the work?

A tool can change a task overnight. Agreeing who decides, what good work looks like, and what people need takes a different kind of attention.

That is where I work: between the technology, the people, and the way an organization makes decisions. We make room for the uncertainty, then turn it into something people can work with.
