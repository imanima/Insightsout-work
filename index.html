The human side of AI change

AI change is bigger than AI training.

AI is reshaping roles, decisions, and how people work together. InsightsOut creates community workshops, organizational programs, and research to help people navigate the human and ethical questions inside that change.

Our approach

Understand. Align. Lead. Enable.

AI changes roles, decisions, confidence, and responsibility. People need more than instructions for using a tool.

Understand

Make sense of what AI is changing for the work and the people doing it.

Align

Agree on priorities, responsibilities, boundaries, and what should stay human.

Lead

Equip leaders and managers to communicate clearly and support people through change.

Enable

Put the choices into practice through workshops and real-work AI labs.
