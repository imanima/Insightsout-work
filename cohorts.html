How a session works

The topic changes. The container stays.

Arrive and connect

A few minutes to land, see each other, and say what is alive this week.

Bring a real situation

Each session has a theme, but the material is yours: the decision, the conversation, the pressure you are carrying.

Coaching, with witnesses

One or two people get coached in the room. Everyone else listens without fixing, and usually learns as much.

Leave with one step

Each person names one small commitment. We start the next session there.

Group agreements: what is said in the group stays in the group. Support over fixing. Connection over competition. No pitching.

Questions

Before you join.

How is a cohort different from a workshop?

A workshop is one session. A cohort is the same small group over weeks or months, so trust builds, you try things between sessions, and you come back to say what happened. It also includes private 1:1 time with me.

When do the cohorts start?

All three start in January 2027. Enrollment opens November 1. Apply for a seat and I will share the dates and the schedule, usually on a short call.

What does a seat cost?

I share the price on the free call. The manager and role change seats are sized to fit a typical learning stipend, and every seat comes with an invoice and receipt for reimbursement.

Do I need to be in San Francisco?

Only for Founders' Inner Circle, which meets in person. The manager and role change cohorts meet online.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Group agreements start with confidentiality, and you never need to share names or company details. If your company pays, it gets an invoice and, if you want, a completion letter. Nothing about what you said.

What if I miss a session?

It happens. Tell me ahead of time if you can, and we can use part of your 1:1 time to catch up.

Can I try the format first?

Yes. The Friday group coaching in San Francisco is a drop-in version of the same format. See the Friday sessions →
