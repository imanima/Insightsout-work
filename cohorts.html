How the three months go

Set it, work it, keep it.

Keep it.

Questions

Before you join.

How is this different from a workshop?

A workshop is one session. The cohort is the same eight people for three months, so trust builds, you try things between sessions, and you come back to say what happened. And you get a private 1:1 with me every month.

When does it start, and what does it cost?

It starts in January 2027 and runs for three months. The first month is free. After that it is priced to fit a typical learning stipend, and I share the number on the free call. Every seat comes with an invoice and receipt, so your company may cover it.

What does first month free mean?

You take part fully for four weeks: the weekly sessions and your first 1:1. At the end of the month you decide. If it is not for you, you leave and owe nothing.

Is it online?

Yes. The weekly sessions are online and last 90 minutes. The time is set before January, after the calls, so it works around a job.

Who is in the group?

Up to eight people: working professionals, managers, and people building something on the side. Everyone brings their own goal. Hearing how someone in a different seat handles the same kind of pressure is part of what makes the group useful.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Group agreements start with confidentiality, and you never need to share names or company details. If your company pays, it gets an invoice and, if you want, a completion letter. Nothing about what you said.

What if I miss a session?

It happens. Tell me ahead of time if you can, and we can use part of your 1:1 time to catch up.
