Inside a session

Real life is the starting point.

A decision that keeps circling. A conversation you’re avoiding. A step you meant to take.

We arrive, check in, and work with what people bring. Some sessions include individual coaching in the group; others make space for shared reflection and practice.

We listen before offering perspective. You choose what to share. Each session ends by identifying something you want to carry into your week.

Shared agreements: respect confidentiality, make room for different experiences, and keep the group free of pitching.

Before you join

A few practical questions.

Who are the groups for?

Working professionals navigating career or leadership changes, and founders or people building a project alongside their job. We consider your situation and goals when suggesting a group.

When and where do groups meet?

We’re forming the next groups now. Start dates, meeting frequency, online or in-person format, and private-session allocation are confirmed on the introductory call and in your written offer before enrollment.

What does the three-month commitment mean?

After the two complimentary introductory sessions, you can choose to enroll in a paid three-month coaching program. Attending the introductory sessions does not automatically enroll or charge you.

How does the November offer work?

Enroll by November 30, 2026, at 11:59 PM Pacific to receive 30% off the full three-month group program. We provide the regular price, discounted total, session schedule, and payment terms before you enroll. This offer applies to group programs, not standalone private coaching.

Are the two free sessions part of the paid program?

They are introductory group sessions before you decide on the three-month commitment. We’ll discuss availability and arrange your introduction on the call.

Can my employer cover the program?

Your employer may have a learning or professional-development budget. Check your policy. We can provide a program description, invoice, and receipt. How to ask your employer →
